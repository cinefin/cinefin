A theater at home.

Turn your digital film collection into functioning theater! Cinefin manages and plays trailers, ratings cards, custom pre-roll and all sorts of user media. Build out programmes, schedule screenings, print tickets, and run a theater at home.

Coming soon for Linux & Windows

Free and open-source software · release news only · privacy

Current Features

  • ✓Sync your movie libraries from Plex and Jellyfin
  • ✓Builds programmes: trailers, pre-roll, home automation, movies
  • ✓Playout via MPV: The player comes embedded or you can connect your own instance (local or network)
  • ✓Custom pre-roll for your own media, ads or announcements
  • ✓Configurable trailer matching that match genre, year and certification of upcoming movies
  • ✓Fully configurable through the use of re-usable programme tempplates
  • ✓Design and load title cards for screenings
  • ✓Home Assistant and generic REST integration for home automation
  • ✓Generates ratings cards for MPAA or BBFC
  • ✓Kiosk mode for front of house
  • ✓Remote control from your phone
  • ✓Prints tickets on a thermal printer(!)
